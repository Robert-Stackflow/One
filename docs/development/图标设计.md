# One 应用图标

日期：2026-10-01。

采用开放的 **O** 与浅绿色 **1** 组成一个标记。深灰底、暖白主形、浅绿强调，与 One 的克制界面一致。没有阴影、渐变、细线或文字；圆角方形之外透明，内部底板保持实色。

## 文件与尺寸

| 文件 | 用途 |
| --- | --- |
| `assets/icons/one.svg` | 标准矢量源，可任意缩放 |
| `assets/icons/one-small.svg` | 小尺寸光学校正源；轮廓稍厚、缝隙简化 |
| `assets/icons/one.ico` | Windows 多尺寸图标：16、20、24、28、32、36、40、48、56、64、96、128、256 |
| `assets/icons/one-{size}.png` | 上述尺寸及 512、1024；共 15 档，透明背景 |
| `assets/icons/concept.png` | 生成工具的概念稿，仅供设计溯源，不参与运行时 |

16、20、24 px 从小尺寸源导出，其余从标准源导出。托盘为 100%、125%、150%、175%、200%、225%、250%、300%、350%、400% 缩放提供对应位图表示。EXE 使用多帧 ICO，窗口使用 PNG，侧栏使用矢量图。

运行 `npm run icons` 可从两个 SVG 重建 PNG 和 ICO。导出时检查外角透明、中心实色，避免把 O 的中间误切成透明洞。

## 制作过程

概念探索使用内置 `image_gen`，未调用 API/CLI。生成稿随后整理为可维护的 SVG 几何形状，并从矢量源按目标尺寸重新栅格化；生产文件不是将一张大位图反复缩小。16–24 px 使用独立的光学校正版本。

颜色为 `#252c29`（深灰）、`#f4f5ef`（暖白）、`#b3cbb8`（浅绿）。首次生成稿的中心透明瑕疵在第二轮提示中明确要求修正；最终生产矢量的内部均为实色。

## 生成提示词记录

初稿（内置工具，透明背景）：

> Use case: logo-brand. Create one final Windows desktop application icon for a local utility named One. Square asset, front-facing flat vector-like geometry, genuinely transparent exterior. A charcoal rounded-square tile, generous smooth rounded corners, centered, with a clean warm-white geometric O loop and a subtle integrated sage-green vertical accent suggesting the numeral 1 on the right side of the O. The loop and accent form one simple cohesive monogram, not separate scattered symbols. Restrained and polished, contemporary desktop software, no resemblance to the OpenAI knot. Strong silhouette, thick strokes, generous negative space; must remain recognizable at 16px, 24px, 32px, and 256px. Use only charcoal #252c29, warm white #f4f5ef, and muted light sage #b3cbb8. No lettering or app name, no extra text, no hairlines, no tiny details, no border decorations, no bevel, no gloss, no shadows outside the icon, no perspective, no scene or mockup, no multiple icons or presentation grid. The tile should occupy about 90% of the square canvas with even transparent margins. A single centered finished application icon.

修改稿（内置工具，以初稿为输入，透明背景）：

> Edit this application icon to be a precise flat vector-style production icon. Keep the same overall O + 1 monogram, dark rounded-square tile, white O, and sage numeral 1. CRITICAL: the rounded-square tile must be a single fully opaque uniform solid charcoal #252c29 plate, INCLUDING the entire center/counter of the O. Fill the irregular black/transparent center hole with exactly the same opaque charcoal as the tile. Transparency is permitted ONLY outside the outer rounded square. Remove absolutely all shading, gradients, bevels, grain, roughness, glow and inner shadows. Use only three perfectly flat solid colors: charcoal #252c29, warm white #f4f5ef, sage #b3cbb8. Refine the shapes into clean simple geometric curves: the O should have an even thick white band and a smooth uniform rounded-rectangular counter, with a clean opening on the right occupied by a sage number 1 with a short angled head and straight thick vertical stem. Keep broad strokes and spacious negative space so the monogram is legible at 16px. No outline or black shadow around the 1; a small clean charcoal gap is enough. Make the whole square composition perfectly centered with even 6 percent transparent padding. Exactly one standalone icon, front view, no words, no labels, no mockup.
